# Turn-based over real-time

We considered a continuous/idle-style simulation (resources ticking in real time, no discrete turns) as an alternative to the GDD's turn-based design. We kept discrete, player-paced turns with a manual "End Turn" button, since the "chill, no timers, no real-time pressure" goal (§10 of the GDD) depends on nothing advancing without the player choosing to act.
